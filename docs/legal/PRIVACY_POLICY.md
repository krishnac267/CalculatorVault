# Privacy Policy — Calculator Vault

**Effective date:** June 7, 2026  
**App:** Calculator Vault (`com.calculator.vault`)

## Summary

Calculator Vault stores your vault data **locally on your device**. We do not operate a cloud vault server. Third-party services (Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Google Play Billing) may collect data as described below.

## Data we store on your device

- Hashed PIN credentials (never plaintext)
- Vault app bookmarks (package names, labels)
- Private notes and bookmarks
- Security settings and session metadata
- Intruder log timestamps and optional local photo paths
- Encrypted Room database (SQLCipher)

## Data we do not collect directly

- Your PIN in plaintext
- Contents of third-party apps you launch from the vault

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Provider |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Firebase Analytics | Usage metrics, retention, feature events | Google |
| Google AdMob | Banner ads on free tier (not on calculator/PIN screens) | Google |
| Google Play Billing | Premium subscriptions and lifetime purchase | Google |

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Camera | Optional intruder photo capture after failed PIN attempts |
| Internet | Ads, analytics, billing |
| AD_ID | AdMob / analytics on supported devices |

## Children's privacy

Calculator Vault is not directed at children under 13.
